Offline Photo Shrink - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 8, 2026

1. Privacy Policy Overview

This privacy policy explains how our mobile application "Offline Photo Shrink" handles your personal information. We believe in complete transparency and maximum privacy, which is why this application is designed to operate without collecting or transferring your private data.

2. Zero Personal Data Collection

This application operates 100% offline. We do NOT collect, save, monitor, or transmit any of your personal data, images, files, or EXIF metadata. All image compression and resizing tasks are processed locally on your device, and are deleted from local cache as soon as the session ends.

Furthermore, this application uses the Android native Photo Picker, which means we do not request or have access to your full device storage or gallery. We only access the specific photo you choose to compress.

3. Third-Party Services (Advertising & In-App Purchases)

To keep this application free, we integrate Google AdMob to display ads. We also use Google Play Billing for premium upgrades. These third-party SDKs may collect and use anonymous identifiers (such as Advertising IDs and Purchase Tokens) to deliver ads, process transactions, and prevent fraud. 4. Policy Changes and Contact

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. If you have any questions, please contact us via the developer contact page on the Google Play Store.
