Offline Photo Shrink - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 3, 2026

1. Privacy Policy Overview

This privacy policy explains how our mobile application "Offline Photo Shrink" handles your personal information. We believe in complete transparency and maximum privacy, which is why this application is designed to operate without collecting or transferring your private data.

2. Zero Personal Data Collection

This application operates 100% offline. We do NOT collect, save, monitor, or transmit any of your personal data, images, files, or EXIF metadata. All image compression and resizing tasks are processed locally on your device, and are deleted from local cache as soon as the session ends.

Furthermore, this application uses the Android native Photo Picker, which means we do not request or have access to your full device storage or gallery. We only access the specific photo you choose to compress.

3. Third-Party Services (Advertising)

To keep this application free, we integrate Google AdMob to display ads. Google AdMob may collect and use anonymous identifiers (such as Advertising IDs) to deliver personalized ads and analyze performance. Please check Google's policies for details:

  • Google Privacy & Terms
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

4. Policy Changes and Contact

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. If you have any questions, please contact us via the developer contact page on the Google Play Store.

1. プライバシーポリシーの概要

本プライバシーポリシーは、モバイルアプリケーション「Offline Photo Shrink」におけるお客様の情報の取り扱いについて説明するものです。私たちはプライバシーを最優先に考えており、本アプリは個人情報の収集や送信を一切行わない設計となっています。

2. 個人情報の「非収集」について

本アプリは100%オフラインで動作します。お客様の画像データ、個人情報、位置情報（GPSなどのEXIFメタデータ）を収集、保存、または外部サーバーに送信することは一切ありません。すべての画像圧縮処理は端末内でローカルに実行され、セッション終了後に一時フォルダから自動消去されます。

また、本アプリはAndroid標準のセキュアなフォトピッカーを採用しているため、お客様の端末ストレージ全体やギャラリー全体へのアクセス権限を要求しません。お客様が明示的に選択した写真のみを一時的に処理します。

3. サードパーティサービス（広告配信）

本アプリは無料提供を維持するため、広告配信にGoogle AdMobを使用しています。Google AdMobは、広告の配信、分析、パーソナライズのために、端末の広告ID等の匿名の識別子を収集する場合があります。これらのデータ収集に関する詳細は、Google社のプライバシーポリシーをご確認ください。

  • Google プライバシーポリシー

1. Visão Geral da Política de Privacidade

Esta política de privacidade explica como o nosso aplicativo móvel "Offline Photo Shrink" lida com as suas informações pessoais. Acreditamos na transparência total e privacidade máxima, por isso este aplicativo opera sem coletar ou transferir seus dados privados.

2. Coleta Zero de Dados Pessoais

Este aplicativo opera 100% offline. Nós NÃO coletamos, salvamos, monitoramos ou transmitimos nenhum dos seus dados pessoais, imagens, arquivos ou metadados EXIF. Todo o processo de compactação de imagem é feito localmente no seu dispositivo.

Além disso, o aplicativo utiliza o seletor de fotos seguro nativo do Android, o que significa que não solicitamos nem temos acesso total ao armazenamento do seu dispositivo. Só acessamos as fotos específicas que você escolhe compactar.
